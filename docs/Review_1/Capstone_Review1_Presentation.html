Problem Statement & Core Industry Challenges

1. Massive Financial Losses (₹3.3 Lakh Crore)

Digital payment fraud causes over ₹3.3 Lakh Crore ($40B+) in annual losses worldwide, severely threatening high-volume Indian UPI, IMPS, and banking gateways with mounting operational risk.

2. Vulnerability of Legacy Rule Engines

Traditional banking security relies on manual static rules that fail to detect novel zero-day fraud tactics, adaptive criminal behaviors, and dynamic multi-account collusive attacks.

3. Severe ~3.5% Fraud Data Imbalance

Because genuine transactions dominate (~96.5%), standard AI models suffer from severe majority-class bias, missing real fraud or causing high false-positive rejections for legitimate users.

4. Lack of Regulatory Transparency

Black-box deep learning models fail to provide clear, human-understandable explanations for credit or loan rejections, violating strict RBI, FCRA, and GDPR regulatory compliance laws.

Real-World Motivation

Multi-Crore Financial Impact

Indian digital payment networks (UPI, IMPS, Cards) process billions of transactions monthly. Undetected fraud breaches cause direct multi-Crore losses to banks and destroy customer trust.

Collusive Fraud Rings

Organized criminal syndicates operate complex inter-account networks to launder illicit funds across banks. Standard tabular classifiers fail to identify these multi-node graph relationships.

RBI Compliance & Audit Trails

Financial regulators strictly require explainable feature attributions (SHAP values) whenever loan requests or credit card applications are flagged or rejected to ensure complete audit compliance.

Sub-100ms Live Execution

High-speed payment gateways demand instantaneous real-time inference under 100 milliseconds to block fraudulent payment attempts before transaction finalization.

Objectives and Industry Applications

5-Model Ensemble Core Objectives

1. Sub-100ms Inference API Gateway
Architect high-throughput FastAPI REST microservices serving real-time risk predictions for high-volume banking payment gateways.
2. Graph Neural Ring Mining (PyTorch GAT)
Construct multi-account transaction graphs to uncover organized money laundering rings & syndicate accounts across banks.
3. Synthetic Data Balancing (CTGAN)
Generate privacy-preserving synthetic transaction samples to balance the severe ~3.5% minority fraud distribution to 50:50.
4. Regulatory Explainability & Audit Logs (SHAP)
Compute instant Shapley feature attributions to satisfy RBI, FCRA, and GDPR transparency & auditability requirements.

Industry Applications & Deployment

1. Real-Time UPI & Credit Card Interception
Instant transaction risk scoring prior to payment settlement on merchant gateways & card processing networks.
2. Automated Digital Lending & Credit Risk (INR ₹)
Dynamic credit risk scoring (0–100 scale) for personal loan approval automation & default prevention.
3. Enterprise Anti-Money Laundering (AML)
Continuous graph-based fraud ring monitoring for high-frequency financial institutions and regulatory agencies.
4. Core Banking & NetBanking Protection
Real-time anomaly filtering for high-value IMPS, NEFT, and RTGS electronic fund transfers.

Literature Review & Research Gap

Paper Approach Strength Limitation
Arik et al., 2021 – TabNet Sequential attention for tabular feature selection High tabular interpretability & feature selection Higher computational overhead than LightGBM
Li et al., 2022 – ECOD (IEEE) Empirical cumulative distribution scoring Parameter-free, replaces legacy Isolation Forest Does not capture inter-account graph relationships
Veličković et al., 2018 – GAT Self-attention over node graph structures Mines multi-account collusive fraud rings Requires graph construction pipeline overhead
Xu et al., 2019 – CTGAN Conditional GANs for imbalanced tabular data Generates high-fidelity synthetic fraud samples Requires careful tuning to avoid mode collapse
Research Gap Identified:

• No prior financial fraud study unifies LightGBM + TabNet + AutoEncoder + ECOD + PyTorch GAT in a single real-time ensemble with CTGAN balancing and SHAP explainability under <100ms constraints.

CORE INNOVATION PILLARS

Novelty of the Idea – What Makes Our Project Unique

Hybrid 5-Model Ensemble

Combines tabular speed (LightGBM), deep attention (TabNet), zero-day anomalies (AutoEncoder), empirical cumulative outliers (ECOD), and graph rings (GAT).

Generative Data Balancing (CTGAN)

Solves severe ~3.5% class imbalance via privacy-preserving synthetic transaction generation, expanding fraud coverage without overfitting.

Explainable AI & Audit Logging (SHAP)

Provides instant Shapley feature attributions for full RBI, FCRA & GDPR compliance on flagged and rejected loan or transaction attempts.

Production MLOps & Drift Monitoring

Continuous automated data drift & concept decay monitoring powered by Evidently AI and FastAPI microservice integration.
