🧠 Mathematical Multi-Head Attention Formulation

For node \(i\) and its relational neighbor \(j \in \mathcal{N}_i\), the multi-head attention coefficient \(\alpha_{ij}^k\) is computed as:

e_{ij} = \text{LeakyReLU}(a^T [W h_i \,\|\, W h_j]) \alpha_{ij} = \frac{\exp(e_{ij})}{\sum_{k \in \mathcal{N}_i} \exp(e_{ik})} h_i' = \sigma \left( \sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}_i} \alpha_{ij} W h_j \right)

🛡️ Why GAT Beats Tabular Models in Card-Testing Attacks

Tabular models (LightGBM/XGBoost) evaluate each row in isolation. If a fraud botnet performs micro-transactions of $5.00 using slightly different timestamps, individual tabular probabilities remain low.

The Graph Attention Network aggregates neighborhood embeddings across the shared card1 and addr1 entity nodes. When sudden multi-edge fan-outs occur, attention weights spike to >0.90, immediately neutralizing the attack ring.
