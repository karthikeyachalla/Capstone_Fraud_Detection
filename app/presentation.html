04 // SYSTEM DESIGN & PIPELINE
Methodology & Architecture: The H-GATBoost Pipeline
Dual-stream neuro-symbolic framework fusing continuous Graph Attention embeddings with cost-sensitive gradient boosted trees.
METHODOLOGY
DUAL-STREAM LATENT FUSION
The Neuro-Symbolic Hybrid Rationale
  • 1. Spatial Representation: 4-Head GAT extracts 32-dim relational risk embeddings from 84,218 multi-relational edges without hard decision boundaries.
  • 2. Bypasses Over-Smoothing: Pure GAT achieves 72.1% recall but collapses to 6.8% precision on merchant hubs; tree splits restore crisp boundaries.
  • 3. +71.9% Precision Lift: H-GATBoost elevates precision to 53.88% (PR-AUC 0.5984) while capturing complex collusive rings.
  • 4. Sub-10ms Bank Serving: Graph embeddings pre-computed in Redis (1.8ms); ONNX tree scoring executes in 4.6ms (< 7ms total latency).
