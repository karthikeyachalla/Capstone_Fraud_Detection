Empirical Motivation & Literature

The Three Crippling Flaws of Modern Banking Fraud Systems

Indian UPI processed 131B transactions in 2024 with an 85% surge in digital financial fraud (RBI Annual Report 2024).

1. Nocturnal Batch Latency

Traditional fraud engines run in T+1 nightly batches. By the time an unauthorized transfer is detected, illicit funds have been laundered across cash ATM networks.

Requires <50ms Real-Time Inline Gateway
2. Transaction Isolation Fallacy

Supervised tabular models evaluate transactions as isolated events. They are blind to collusive multi-account money mule syndicates sharing devices or beneficiary hubs.

Requires Relational Graph Attention (GAT)
3. Black-Box Compliance Trap

Arbitrary model declines violate RBI Master Directions & DPDP Act 2023. Automated adverse actions must provide mathematical, human-readable justification.

Requires Real TreeSHAP Attribution
High-Performance Serving Paradigm

Two-Tier Lambda Serving Architecture (<10ms SLA)

Decoupling heavy graph convolutions from live transaction gateways (citing Alarfaj 2024, Anuganti 2024).

Tier 1: Online Scoring Microservice (<5ms)
  • Cost-Sensitive LightGBM: Asymmetric loss optimization (False Negative penalty >> False Positive friction).
  • Deep PyTorch Autoencoder: Semi-supervised anomaly detector trained only on legitimate flows. Reconstruction MSE flags zero-day attacks.
  • Near-Line Cache Query: Ingests pre-computed 32-dim Graph Mule Embeddings in <1.8ms.
Tier 2: Near-Line Relational GAT (Asynchronous)
  • Bipartite Topology: Links Customer accounts, Beneficiary banks, and Device hardware IDs.
  • Multi-Head Self-Attention: Identifies fan-in money mule hubs and syndicated credential clusters.
  • Fast Embeddings Cache: Pre-computes 32-dim risk vectors into in-memory storage, eliminating inline graph traversal lag.
